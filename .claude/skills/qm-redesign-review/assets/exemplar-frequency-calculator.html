Frequency Calculator, polished

The page answers one question, what frequency is this channel, and today the answer sits before the question. The controls lead, the band detail reads as downlink against uplink, and bands and history share one row on wide screens.

Where it reads rough today

Pins mark a schematic of the page at 1920px with channel 150 calculated, drawn from the code at commit 11b75e46. A real review embeds the screenshot here.

  1. 1
    The answer sits before the questionHERO_SPLIT puts the 19rem readout in the first column, so the eye lands on the result before anything is typed. Its centred stack also sets the row height the form can't fill (Two-Edge).
  2. 2
    A three-word control 1,180px wideFORM.TABS is w-full grid-cols-3, so Auto, LTE and NR get about 390px each and stop reading as one control (Row-Span).
  3. 3
    A 1,040px field for a six-digit numberCalculate floats far from where you type, and type="number" adds a spinner nobody uses.
  4. 4
    About 90px of nothing under the fieldFORM.ROOT is h-full and FORM.NOTE is mt-auto, so the gap is structural and shows in every state (Group-Before-You-Pad).
  5. 5
    Downlink and uplink pair up by accidentBAND_TILE.LIST flows row by row across four columns: Duplex beside DL frequency, Uplink range beside EARFCN range. Downlink range and Uplink range end up about 750px apart.
  6. 6
    Every history row is its own tileHISTORY_ROW.ROOT is rounded-tile bg-surface-container, ten tiles in one card (One-Nest), and each delete sits about 1,450px from the value it deletes.

Proposed layout

This version works: type a channel, switch modes, click a history row to recall it. The band tables cover common EU and APAC bands only, and the history rows are examples.

Kept as is

  • The anchor shell. CALC_HERO radius, padding and header, plus the Cell Scanner link in the meta slot.
  • Motion. The SwapLabel figure swap, the staggerItem page entrance, the band row cascade, and the history exit on quick.
  • State. The browser-saved history capped at HISTORY_LIMIT, and the record rule that a mode switch re-answers without recording.
  • Icons. Every glyph is already in the Material subset, including arrow_downward and arrow_upward. No regeneration needed.

Your call before build

  • Overturning FORM.ROOT. Its comment says h-full "is what lets NOTE sit on the bottom edge instead of leaving a void under the field". It moved the void rather than removing it. Remove the note and let the controls set the height?
  • Machine voice scope. IDENT is shared with the scan tables and the lock-cell dialog. Retell only the calculator now, or the whole family in one pass?
  • Recall on click is new behaviour. Keep it, or leave rows read-only?
  • The position track is new content. Keep it, or ship the table without it?
  • Copy: the readout's idle line, the bands description and the error lines get shorter, and lose their em dashes. All five locale packs move together.
