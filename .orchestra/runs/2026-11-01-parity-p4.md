### Custom DNS: 551E vs RM520N
551E posts `dns1-3` + `dns1v6/dns2v6` to `dns.sh`. RM520N `custom_dns.sh` takes `{enabled, ignore_carrier, servers:"a,b"}`
(max 4, each v4 or v6) and reports `available`/`dnsMode`, `currentUpstream`/`currentSource`, `passthroughBypass`,
`blockCorrupt`. **No wire-format change.**
- Resolver card (On/Off SegmentedField, provider Select, CardFoot Discard + SaveButton) + IPv4 and IPv6 server
  cards as peers, one draft, `form=` join — as 551E.
- Slots: 2 per family (presets are 2+2 and the CGI cap is 4). A saved list that does not fit (e.g. 3 IPv4) shows
  extra slots so nothing is lost; the draft blocks Apply above 4 total. Send `servers = v4 then v6`, trimmed, non-empty.
- RM520N-only facts slotted in (D3): "Ignore carrier DNS" row in the Resolver card; a read-only "Answering now"
  row (current upstream + source); `available=false` → condition block in place of the form; `blockCorrupt` and
  `passthroughBypass` → Banners above the cards. The retired `dns-strip.tsx` goes.
- Provider presets: port `dns-providers.ts` verbatim (Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard, ControlD).
