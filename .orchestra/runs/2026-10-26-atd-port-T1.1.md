# T1.1 — Poller shell wins on RM520N (Phase P1, no broker)
parent: `.orchestra/runs/2026-10-26-atd-poller-port.md`   rev: r1   opened: 2026-10-26   state: AWAITING USER OK

## Goal
Cut the poller's CPU (T0.2: 947 ms per 3.25 s cycle = 29 % of the one core, 85 % of it in forked
children) without the broker, using B5.29's techniques. status.json content unchanged.

## Finding that shapes the plan
Recon (two scouts, 2026-10-26; hunk maps in `.orchestra/scratch/b529_hunk_map.tsv` and
`.orchestra/scratch/dry/`) shows the RM520N poller and its libs have diverged from RM551E:
`parse_serving_cell`, `parse_cgcontrdp`, `conn_state_read`, `events.sh` are rewritten; RM520N has
no heartbeat, no `ondemand_radio.sh`, and bash (not ash) runs the poller. Only qlog.sh and ~9 of 16
parse_at hunks apply as patches. So T1.1 **re-authors the RM520N hot path with B5.29's techniques**,
aimed at the fork sites measured on this poller — it does not apply RM551E patches.

Each slice is measured on the device before the next starts, so a slice that buys nothing is dropped.

## Out of scope (with reason)
- Fixed-clock 2 s period: raises AT cadence from ~3.25 s to 2 s on the single core — a behaviour
  change; decide after S1–S7 numbers (open decision below).
- Heartbeat activity tiers, `ui_active` touch in CGIs: P6.
- `SIP_DELAY` removal and `qcmd_exec_g`: rely on the broker; RM520N's 0.1 s gaps give CGIs a window
  on the flock.
- Busy latch (34c94bd4): needs broker `-t`.
- `ping.env` / `dns.env` / `ondemand.env` sidecars and the `conn_state.sh` reader: RM520N reader is
  jq-based and has 9 sourcers — a redesign, not a port. Revisit only if S7 numbers leave it worth it.
- `qlog.sh` `_qlog_write` hunks: apply cleanly but reach every logging script (27); not a poller
  hot-path cost per T0.2. Separate ticket if wanted.
- events.sh `_ev_net_context` / SCC PCI (D7): the functions differ on RM520N; no measured cost.

## Validation
1. Per slice on RM520N-GL: deploy poller (+ lib) with `modem_write`, restart `qmanager-poller`
   (**needs your OK — one approval for the T1.1 restarts**), then the T0.2 M4 method for 300 s:
   CPU per cycle, period, share of core.
2. Parity: capture 50 status.json snapshots before and after each slice; compare under `jq -S`
   minus `timestamp`/time fields. For S7, a one-off script in `.orchestra/scratch/` (not committed)
   runs `write_cache` and `write_cache_jq` on the same globals 200× and diffs them.
3. Gates: `bash -n` poller, `sh -n` libs, CRLF check, md5 deployed vs HEAD, blind `qm-verifier`,
   `busybox-portability-checker` for parse_at.sh (sourced under bash, `#!/bin/sh`).
4. T1.2 (separate): 12 h soak, plus a window with the dashboard open to measure lock contention
   under UI load (the T0.2 gap).

builds S3 and S6, then S7, with the lead reviewing each diff. Rollback: previous poller saved to
