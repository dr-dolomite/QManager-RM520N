## Decision for the user

- **(a) Recommended.** Retire `sms_tool` everywhere now. Keep shipping `atcli_smd11`; install it
  only where `qcmd` stays the shell script; remove it where `qcmd` is the broker link.
- (b) Retire `sms_tool` now; leave `atcli_smd11` exactly as today until T8.1.

(a) gets the RM520N to its end state now and costs one condition; T8.1 then only flips the
allow-list. (b) is smaller but leaves an unused 660 kB binary and a health-check row on the RM520N.
